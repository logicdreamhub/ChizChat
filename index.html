ChizChat

A free peer-to-peer messenger. Your messages, calls and files go straight from your device to theirs, not through a chat server.

No account · No phone number · Android, Windows, Linux

Get it on Google Play Download APK, Windows, Linux Quiver server (open source)

Four ways to reach people

Use any of them, or all at once. Two of them work with no internet at all.

Internet

Friends

Everyday chat, calls and file sharing with people anywhere.

No internet

Nearby

Fast, large file transfers between devices on the same Wi-Fi.

Internet

Rooms

No internet

Workspace

Colleagues and teams chatting over the office LAN.

Everything a messenger should do

Plus tools for small businesses that sell over chat.

  • Voice and video calls, screen sharing
  • Voice messages, photos, files and whole folders
  • Read receipts and QR-code pairing
  • Calls ring over the lock screen, even when the app is closed
  • Labels, "Needs reply" and quick replies
  • Contact notes, follow-up reminders and scheduled messages
  • Business profile with opening hours, away and greeting messages
  • Product catalog and payment cards, order stages from New to Delivered

Private by design

There is no ChizChat account, so there is nothing to sign in to and nothing to leak.

Device to device

Messages and files travel directly between devices whenever they can reach each other, encrypted in transit.

Relay can't read

When a direct link isn't possible, an encrypted relay passes traffic along without reading or storing it.

Offline delivery you can own

End-to-end encrypted mailbox via Quiver, ours or your own server. Unread mail is deleted after 30 days.

Your history stays local

Chats, labels and notes live on your device, backed up only with a password you choose.
